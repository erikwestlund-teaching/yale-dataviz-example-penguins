# Palmer Penguins

One row represents one penguin. `NA` means a missing value.

| Variable | Meaning |
|---|---|
| species | Penguin species |
| island | Island where the penguin was observed |
| bill_length_mm | Bill length in millimeters |
| bill_depth_mm | Bill depth in millimeters |
| flipper_length_mm | Flipper length in millimeters |
| body_mass_g | Body mass in grams |
| sex | Recorded sex |
| year | Observation year |

Source: [palmerpenguins](https://allisonhorst.github.io/palmerpenguins/), by Allison Horst, Alison Hill, and Kristen Gorman. The bundled CSV is unchanged from the package's `inst/extdata/penguins.csv`. Data are distributed under CC0.
